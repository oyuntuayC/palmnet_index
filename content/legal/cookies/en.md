## 1. Introduction

This Cookies Policy explains how Palmnet Tech SL (“**PalmNet**”, “**we**”, “**us**”) uses cookies and similar technologies on palmnet.ai and related online properties. It should be read together with our [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy).

## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, and software development kits (SDKs) in mobile apps. They help websites function, remember preferences, and understand how services are used.

## 3. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

- Keep you signed in and protect accounts
- Remember language and display preferences
- Understand traffic and improve website performance
- Measure marketing campaign effectiveness where permitted
- Maintain security and detect abuse

## 4. Types of Cookies We Use

### 4.1 Strictly necessary cookies
Required for core functions such as security, authentication, load balancing, and locale routing. These cookies do not require consent under applicable EU rules, but we still disclose them here.

### 4.2 Functional cookies
Remember choices such as preferred language or UI settings to provide a more personalized experience.

### 4.3 Analytics cookies
Help us understand how visitors use our site (for example, pages viewed and approximate geography) so we can improve content and performance. These are used only where permitted (including after consent where required).

### 4.4 Marketing cookies
May be used to measure or deliver relevant campaigns. We use them only with consent where required by law.

## 5. Cookie Duration

- **Session cookies** expire when you close your browser
- **Persistent cookies** remain for a defined period or until deleted

Retention periods vary by purpose and provider. We aim to keep durations no longer than necessary.

## 6. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by third parties that provide hosting, analytics, or embedded content. Those providers process data under their own policies. We select providers carefully and configure them to limit unnecessary collection where feasible.

## 7. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through:

- **Browser settings** — block or delete cookies (note that blocking necessary cookies may break site functions)
- **Consent tools** — where we present a cookie banner or preference center, you can update choices at any time
- **Platform settings** — for mobile apps, review OS privacy and notification settings

Popular browsers provide cookie controls in Settings / Privacy. You may also use industry opt-out tools where available for advertising cookies.

## 8. Mobile Apps

Our mobile apps may use device identifiers, SDKs, and local storage for authentication, diagnostics, and optional analytics/push notifications. App Store and Google Play privacy labels describe categories of data linked to the app. In-app permissions are requested only when needed for features you use.

## 9. Updates

We may update this Cookies Policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised accordingly.
