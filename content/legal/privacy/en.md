## 1. Introduction

Palmnet Tech SL (“**PalmNet**”, “**we**”, “**us**”, or “**our**”) provides restaurant technology products and services, including POS checkout, online ordering, self-ordering kiosks, kitchen display (KDS), membership and marketing tools, advertising displays, and related mobile applications (collectively, the “**Services**”).

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, with whom we share it, and the rights you have. It applies to our websites (including palmnet.ai), merchant dashboards, and mobile apps published on the App Store and other platforms.

By using the Services, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

## 3. Data We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

### 3.1 Account and contact information
- Name, email address, phone number, company/store name, and job title
- Login credentials and authentication data
- Support and sales inquiry content

### 3.2 Merchant and store data
- Store profiles, menus, pricing, inventory settings, device configurations
- Staff accounts and role permissions you create in the Services

### 3.3 Orders, payments, and transactions
- Order details, tickets, modifiers, loyalty events, and related timestamps
- Payment status and references (card numbers are processed by payment providers; we do not store full card PAN data)

### 3.4 Device and app information
- Device model, OS version, app version, language, and time zone
- Device identifiers used for security, diagnostics, and push notifications (where enabled)
- Crash logs and performance diagnostics

### 3.5 Usage, logs, and analytics
- Feature usage events, session metadata, IP address, and approximate location derived from IP
- Server logs needed for security, fraud prevention, and troubleshooting

### 3.6 Cookies and similar technologies
- Necessary cookies for authentication and security
- Optional analytics or preference cookies where permitted  
  See our [Cookies Policy](/en/cookies) for details.

We do not knowingly collect special categories of personal data (such as health or biometric data) through the Services unless you explicitly provide them and we have a lawful basis to process them.

## 4. How We Use Personal Data

We use personal data to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain the Services
- Create and manage merchant and user accounts
- Process orders, sync kitchen workflows, and enable membership/marketing features you configure
- Process payments through integrated payment partners
- Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
- Improve product reliability, performance, and user experience
- Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security incidents
- Comply with legal, tax, and regulatory obligations
- Send service-related notices (for example, security alerts or material product changes)
- Send marketing communications where permitted by law and where you have not opted out

## 5. Legal Bases (GDPR)

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we rely on one or more of the following bases:

- **Contract performance** — to provide the Services you request
- **Legitimate interests** — to secure, improve, and administer our products, provided these interests are not overridden by your rights
- **Consent** — for optional cookies, certain analytics, and marketing where required
- **Legal obligation** — to meet accounting, tax, and regulatory requirements

You may withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

## 6. Sharing of Personal Data

We may share personal data with:

- **Service providers** that host infrastructure, email, customer support, analytics, or similar functions under contract
- **Payment processors and acquiring partners** to complete transactions
- **Professional advisors** (legal, accounting) where needed
- **Authorities** when required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety
- **Business transfer parties** in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale (subject to appropriate safeguards)

We do not sell personal data.

Where we process restaurant guest data on behalf of a merchant, we share or expose that data only as instructed by the merchant and as needed to operate the Services.

## 7. International Transfers

PalmNet is established in Spain (European Union). Personal data may be processed in the EU/EEA and, where necessary, in other countries by our service providers.

When we transfer personal data outside the EEA/UK to a country without an adequacy decision, we use appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and additional technical/organizational measures as needed.

## 8. Retention

We retain personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy, including:

- Account and contract data: for the duration of the commercial relationship and a reasonable period thereafter for disputes and legal claims
- Billing and tax records: as required by applicable law
- Support tickets and logs: for a limited period needed for operations and security
- Marketing contacts: until you unsubscribe or object

When retention ends, we delete or anonymize the data, unless a longer period is required by law.

## 9. Security

We implement technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data, including access controls, encryption in transit where appropriate, monitoring, and staff confidentiality obligations. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we continuously work to improve our safeguards.

## 10. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law (including GDPR), you may have the right to:

- Access your personal data
- Rectify inaccurate data
- Erase data (“right to be forgotten”)
- Restrict or object to certain processing
- Receive data portability
- Withdraw consent
- Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos — AEPD)

To exercise these rights, contact us at [info@palmnet.co](mailto:info@palmnet.co). We may need to verify your identity before responding.

If you are an end guest of a restaurant using PalmNet, please contact that restaurant first for requests about orders or loyalty data they control.

## 11. Children’s Privacy

The Services are intended for business users and restaurant operations. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16 (or under 13 where that lower age applies under local law). If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.
